# Judge dimension: aesthetics

Inspect the rendered figure before you score.
Score 1 when consistent typography, alignment, spacing, hierarchy, and restrained colors produce a coherent figure.
Score 0 for conflicting styles, arbitrary emphasis, unbalanced groups, or decorative elements that obscure the structure.
Accept simple technical diagrams. Do not require pictorial artwork.
Judge the composition rather than a preferred palette or renderer.
Use `unknown: true` when the preview does not permit a visual assessment.
Write the reason in Simplified Technical English.
